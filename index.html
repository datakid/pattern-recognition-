Veritas Lens

Pattern recognition · 100% on-device · nothing uploaded

Notes
  • Can read: C2PA Content Credentials (ChatGPT/DALL·E/Sora, Adobe Firefly, Microsoft, Google), IPTC trainedAlgorithmicMedia, generator text in PNG files (Stable Diffusion, ComfyUI), EXIF, XMP and ID3 tags.
  • Cannot read: Google SynthID and other invisible watermarks. Only the vendor can decode them.
  • C2PA is verified: the manifest is parsed, the COSE signature is checked with WebCrypto, and the content hash is recomputed over the file. The certificate chain is then checked against the official C2PA trust list (bundled). Revocation is not checked. MP4/MOV box hashes are recomputed for unfragmented files.
  • JPEG forensics: where the quantization tables came from, the IJG quality, 8×8 grid alignment, double quantization and a JPEG ghost map.
  • Video: fixed sensor noise carried across frames, texture shimmer in still areas, and brightness flicker.
  • Screenshots, re-saves and messaging apps strip metadata and weaken pixel traces, so reliability goes down.
  • Honest scoring: only measured, objective signals change the score. Filename, clip length, saturation and duration are still shown, but they add 0 points. If no provenance tag is found, the score stays between 15 and 85, and it is halved when the pixel signals disagree.
  • Pixel checks include noise residual, kurtosis, CFA, a 1D and 2D FFT spectrum, JPEG grid, histogram comb gaps, colour diversity and copy-move clone detection.
  • Video reliability is capped at 45% and audio at 60%, unless a provenance tag is found.

Camera match · sensor fingerprint (PRNU)

Every camera sensor leaves a faint, fixed noise pattern. Build a fingerprint from photos you know came from one camera, then test whether other photos share it. Works on original, uncropped, unresized files at least 512 px across. Use 10–20 bright, plain references such as sky or walls.

Notes
  • Recognizes amounts, quantities with units, percentages, dates, times, emails, URLs, phones, IPs, UUIDs, IBANs, card numbers, coordinates, hex colours, versions, IDs, hashtags and mentions.
  • Values are checked, not just matched: Luhn for cards, mod-97 for IBANs, real calendar dates, IP octet range, coordinate range. Number-like strings that fail Luhn are not listed as cards.
  • Each numeric column gets min, median, max and sum, and outliers are flagged with a robust MAD z-score above 3.5.
  • Groups lines that share the same shape into tables. Also handles delimited tables and repeating key:value records.
  • Junk is removed automatically: HTML, dividers, duplicates, boilerplate like "Sent from my…" or "Unsubscribe", and invisible characters. Everything removed is listed with the reason.
  • Writing check (English, 150+ words): marker vocabulary, stock phrases, sentence and paragraph burstiness, informal slips, leftover Markdown and self-disclosure. The score stays between 15 and 85 unless the text discloses itself (for example "as an AI language model"). Reliability is capped at 65%. It is never proof of authorship.
  • Rule-based, with no AI model, so prose with unusual formatting stays as plain text.

What that means for a result here

  • Verified C2PA with a trusted chain: as strong as any online tool.
  • Writing check: comparable signals to the vocabulary studies; never as evidence on its own.
  • Leans AI / Leans camera without a tag: weaker than a trained classifier on known generators, but the reasons are visible and checkable.
  • Inconclusive is a real answer: no tool can prove a clean, stripped image is authentic.
